MicSentry: Anti-Spy & Mic Guard — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Commitment

MicSentry operates 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, audio recordings, camera images, network logs, Bluetooth identifiers, or device analytics to any external server.

2. How Hardware Permissions Are Used Locally

  • Local Network & Bluetooth: Used locally on your device when you run Room Sweep to measure signal strength (RSSI) and identify cameras, speakers, or trackers in your immediate vicinity.
  • Camera: Used locally in real time when you open the Optical IR & Lens Scanner to apply color-contrast filters. No photos or videos are saved or uploaded.
  • Motion / Magnetometer: Used locally to measure magnetic flux (µT) at close range (2–15 cm) to detect concealed speakers or coils.
  • Microphone (Strictly User-Initiated 10s Ultrasonic Sweep): MicSentry never records voice or accesses audio in the background. Microphone input is only sampled for 10 seconds when you manually tap Scan Ultrasonic Beacons (18–22 kHz) to compute high-frequency spectrum energy in memory, which is discarded immediately.
  • No VPN or Traffic Routing: MicSentry does not contain any VPN functionality, proxy servers, or packet tunnels. It never intercepts, routes, or collects network traffic.

3. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding privacy or security, please contact support at: support@micsentry.app
